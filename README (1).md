# AI Inventory, Risk Categorization, and Governance Gap Assessment

A practical, five-phase approach to establishing an AI inventory, categorizing risk, assessing governance controls, and identifying governance gaps — mapped to the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, UK AI Regulation Principles, and the OECD AI Principles.

📄 **Full project:** [AI-Inventory-Governance-Gap-Assessment.md](AI-Inventory-Governance-Gap-Assessment.md)

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Frameworks** | EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, UK AI Regulation Principles, OECD AI Principles |
| **Sample systems inventoried** | Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, LinkedIn AI Features, AI Recruitment Tool, Customer Service Chatbot |
| **Governance areas assessed** | Human oversight, transparency, privacy, security, accountability |
| **Deliverables** | AI Inventory Register, AI Risk Register, Governance Gap Assessment Report, Executive Summary Dashboard |

## The Five Phases

1. **AI Inventory** — catalog AI systems, purpose, data processed, and users
2. **Risk Categorization** — classify each system as Low, Medium, or High risk
3. **Governance Assessment** — review oversight, transparency, privacy, security, and accountability controls
4. **Governance Gap Analysis** — compare current practice against requirements and flag gaps
5. **Recommendations** — governance, risk management, security, and compliance actions
